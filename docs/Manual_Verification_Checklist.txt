 BEFORE YOU START - TWO THINGS TO KNOW
   1. VisionGuard decides "inside the zone" from the point at the BOTTOM-CENTRE
      of a person's box, roughly their FEET. A person whose body overlaps the
      zone but whose feet are outside it is NOT counted. On a laptop webcam you
      usually see only your upper body, so step back until your whole body is
      visible, or draw the zone so it reaches the bottom edge of the picture.
   2. A zone saved from the portal is stored in  zone.json  and is used by BOTH
      the portal and  main.py.  The built-in zone only fits  vtest.mp4.

 LIMITS TO REMEMBER WHEN YOU SIGN THIS OFF (these are known, not defects)
   - Counts are not guaranteed exact. Tracking IDs are temporary labels; the
     same person can get a new ID and be counted again, or be missed.
   - Only the bottom-centre ("feet") of a box decides inside or outside.
   - A person first seen already inside the zone is not counted.
   - A change must last 3 analysed frames to count (filters one-frame flicker).
   - Using a playback speed skips frames, so a very fast walker crossing a thin
     zone can be missed. "Every frame" is the most exact but may be very slow.
   - Detection speed depends on free memory; with little free memory a video
     can play at a few percent of normal speed unless a playback speed is set.
   - This project was developed and tested on Windows 10 with Python 3.12.7.
   - Results on one sample video do not prove reliable behaviour in other
     lighting, camera angles or crowds.
